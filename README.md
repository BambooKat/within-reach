**Within Reach** lets you reach out to your storages up to 64 blocks away!

Open your inventory, a crafting table or a furnace and a side panel lists everything stored in the chests, barrels, shulker boxes and ender chest around you. No need to remember which chest you put items in, the mod does.

## Features

- **Take items from the panel.** Left click takes a stack and right click takes one. The item goes to your hand, or straight to your inventory with Shift+click.
- **Put items back.** Drop on the panel to put items back in their chest or, if full or inaccessible, in the next nearest storage.
- **Browse and search.** Creative-style category tabs for vanilla and mods and a search bar.
- **See where things are.** The tooltip shows the total and which containers hold the item, using the name you gave them.
- **Furnaces & co. too.** Only finished output is listed, so you never grab the coal/fuel you are cooking with or the uncooked/unsmelted item. Hoppers, dispensers, droppers and some crafting stations are left out on purpose.
- **Fits most screens.** The panel moves the window out of the way when it needs room, and hides itself if there is really no space.
- **Shortcut and button.** Press `G` (rebindable in Controls) or click the chest button next to the inventory.
- **Configurable reach.** 1 to 64 blocks (default 16), from Mod Menu or `config/withinreach.json`.
- Available in English and Italian.

## Good to know

- On a server, install the mod both on the server and on every client. The server's reach setting applies to all.
- Locked containers, trapped chests and unopened loot chests are never listed.
- Containers from other mods that use a standard inventory can be added through the block tag `withinreach:storage`.

## Requirements

Minecraft 26.2, Fabric Loader, Fabric API. Mod Menu is optional.
For multiplayer, install the mod on both the server and the clients.
